My reminder didn't arrive

  • iPhone: allow notifications for HabitBotany (Settings → Notifications → HabitBotany) and check that a Focus mode isn't silencing it. iOS holds a limited number of scheduled reminders, so open the app now and then to refresh them.
  • Android: allow notifications for HabitBotany. Reminders are approximate and follow Android's battery settings.

HabitBotany Pro and rare seeds

  • Restore a purchase: open the shop or Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with.
  • Cancel or change your plan: Settings → Manage subscription, or your Apple Account → Subscriptions / Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Your free garden and all your history stay either way.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google. On iPhone, use Manage subscription in the app or reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

HabitBotany has no account and keeps your garden only on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
