Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026 · Applies to the HabitBotany apps for iOS and Android

HabitBotany ("we", "the app") is published by Long Hung Ta, an independent developer in Vietnam. Contact: hectorta1989@outlook.com.

The short version

  • No account and no sign-up. We don't run a server that stores your data.
  • Your habits, garden, completion history, schedules, reminder times and settings are stored only on your device.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Purchases of HabitBotany Pro and rare seed packs are processed by Apple or Google through RevenueCat, which receives the limited data described below.

Data stored on your device

Habit names, plant species and varieties, the days you watered each habit, schedules, reminder times, archived habits, your garden palette and other preferences. The home-screen widget reads a copy of your garden from storage shared only between the app and its widget on the same device. You can delete all of it by deleting the habits or uninstalling the app. If your operating system backs up app data (for example iCloud or Google backups), it stays inside your own backup.

Reminders

Habit reminders are scheduled locally by your device's notification system. They are not sent from a server, and HabitBotany does not currently send remote push notifications. If we add them later, we will update this page first.

Sharing

Garden and milestone cards are created on your device. They leave your device only when you choose to share them through the system share sheet, and then only to the app or person you pick.

Children

HabitBotany is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. Purchase records held by RevenueCat and the app stores are retained as required for billing, tax and restoring purchases. To ask for deletion of the RevenueCat record linked to your anonymous app user ID, email us. You can find the ID in the app under Settings → Manage subscription.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we don't hold your data on our own servers, most requests can be met by deleting the app's data. For anything else, contact us at the email above.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
